Approve the visual layout as well as the selected product

Large-format porcelain in a villa should be reviewed as a layout decision, not only as a sample decision. Before bulk approval, identify the main rooms, visible transitions, preferred joint direction and any areas where cuts or alignment will be especially noticeable. If the design depends on a stone-look or directional pattern, ask the project team to agree how faces will be mixed and how the visual flow should continue between adjacent spaces.

The buyer should also separate product approval from installation planning. Confirm the selected format and product-specific technical information with the supplier, while the contractor confirms substrate, handling, cutting and movement-joint requirements for the actual site. Keep spare and replacement planning linked to the approved batch or purchase record. This avoids treating a visually similar future product as automatically interchangeable when the original project needs a later replacement. Record the agreed layout assumptions with the sample approval so procurement, installation and future maintenance teams reference the same design decision.
