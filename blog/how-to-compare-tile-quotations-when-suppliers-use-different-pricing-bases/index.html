A practical buyer checkpoint

Before the decision moves forward, create a one-page checkpoint with the item code, application, requested quantity, current quotation version, approved sample reference, packing assumptions, required documents, responsible approver and unresolved questions. Mark each item as confirmed, pending or not applicable. Do not convert a pending point into an assumption simply to keep the schedule moving. If the supplier proposes an alternative, give it a separate line so the commercial and technical differences remain visible.
